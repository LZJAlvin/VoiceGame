Foley Party

Make the noises. Find out what they were.

The host picks a scene from a video. Everyone gets a few seconds of its sound, with no picture, and records their best copy. When everyone's done, the scene plays back with only your sounds on it.

  • 1The host loads a short clip and marks the sound moments.
  • 2Each player hears only their bits and copies them into the mic.
  • 3Nudge your timing until it lines up, then send it.
  • 4Watch the scene together, dubbed by you.

You'll need a microphone. Headphones help a lot.

Waiting for the scene

The host is choosing a scene and marking the sounds.

  • Find a quiet spot and plug in headphones if you have them.
  • You'll hear a few seconds of sound. Copy it with your voice, hands, or anything nearby.
  • You won't see the picture until the end.

Sound parts

Split the soundtrack so each player hears just one part, like only the effects or only the voices. Best: run your clip through mvsep.com with a DnR v3 model (speech, music, sfx) or Demucs (vocals, drums, bass, other), download the parts and add them here; they're lined up automatically. Quick split works without any download but is rough: it separates hits and bangs from tones and voices.
